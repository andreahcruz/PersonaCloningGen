## 3. Schema contract and invariants

`Snapshot` holds `Document`, `AdmissionDecision`, `Passage`, `Span`, `Entity`, `SupportBinding`, `Assertion`, and `Conflict` tuples. `Provenance` records raw/cleaned/record/dataset/split/gold/selection-code hashes and source-line/row/group lineage. Document IDs bind the source/body version; passage/span IDs bind exact offsets and hashes. Assertion IDs bind structured statement, wording and support identity; lifecycle/review changes alone do not change assertion ID. Preserve review history outside an overwritten assertion row.

Initial entity kinds: `person`, `organization`, `product`, `role`, `action`, `concept`. Predicates: **`has_metric`, `has_role`, `recommends`, `discourages`** only. `Statement` carries subject, author speaker, typed object/organization, quantity, polarity, modality, conditions, time and scope. Conditions support flat all/any clauses. `Quantity` binds exact surface/canonical value to subject, metric, time and basis; `quantity_canonical` uses Decimal for USD, percentages, counts and ratios. Metric observations require explicit supported periods; publication time is not an inferred metric period. `unknown` and `not_stated` are different. Unsupported ranges/relative dates/comparisons need quarantine or a separately reviewed schema change, not coercion.

Required invariants:

1. Strict `snapshot_from_dict` rejects unknown fields/types; a supplied `eligible` flag has no authority. Direct dataclass construction is also checked by `validate_snapshot`.
2. Offsets are zero-based Unicode code points, end-exclusive, in the **unaltered decoded cleaned `content`**. Span offsets are document-absolute and must lie within their passage. Hash exact UTF-8 text; never locate spans in normalized prose and reuse those coordinates.
3. Admitted source plus independently annotated `SupportBinding` plus approved assertion review are all necessary. Binding and proposed `Statement` must agree, with spans for every required slot. MVP support comes from one document, not stitched inference. Names/numbers in the query are not evidence.
4. Review uses `admission_subject` or `assertion_subject`, named reviewer, timezone-aware timestamp and reference. Set final assertion lifecycle before computing its review digest. A changed support closure invalidates the old attestation. Humans must check that wording, bindings, negation and endorsement actually follow from the source; hashes cannot establish entailment or authenticate a reviewer.
5. `append_decision` appends per-document hash-linked decisions; do not edit a pending decision in EXP-004. New source/author signals can change the review subject: record a new version/lineage rather than silently altering the prior document.
6. Evidence states are `pending_review`, `eligible`, `quarantined`, `rejected`, `duplicate`, `conflict`. Only results recomputed by `retrievable_assertions(snapshot, bodies, proofs)` are retrievable. Lifecycle `reviewed` alone is insufficient. A stale/unapproved review remains noneligible even if a human-facing label says reviewed.
7. Validate the complete relevant registry before top-k truncation. Duplicate IDs fail closed; equivalent propositions are deduplicated; detected same-scope conflicts and declared conflicts are withheld. Current detection does not discover all linguistic contradictions or overlapping date intervals. Preserve original records and uncertainty; no averaging or automatic “latest wins.”
