# Reducing Churn in B2B SaaS: 3 Practical Ideas

You can't force people to stick around, but you can make it less likely they'll leave.
 
## Don't Solve Problems Nobody Has

The thing about solving problems nobody has is that it's a guarantee of failure. If the founders aren't experiencing the problem themselves, how can they be sure anyone else does?

*   They have to want it themselves
*   It has to be something they can build
*   Few others have to realize its value

## Get Close to Your Customers

You're not just building a product; you're serving people. If your users are unhappy, your churn will skyrocket.

*   Make them happy, rather than just making do with "good enough"
*   Listen to their feedback and incorporate it into your product
*   Be willing to change course if that's what they need

## Don't Take It Personally

The problem isn't the customer; it's that you're not giving them what they want. If someone leaves, don't take it as a personal rejection.

Takeaway: Reducing churn in B2B SaaS is about building a product people want and using their feedback to improve it.
